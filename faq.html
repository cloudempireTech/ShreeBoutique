How do I find out what is available right now?

Message Shree Boutique on WhatsApp or visit its Facebook page for the latest posts. Ask about the specific outfit that interests you.

Can I ask about a piece shown in the gallery?

Yes. Tell the boutique which image caught your eye. The gallery includes real posts and stills from the editorial boutique film; a pictured piece may not currently be in stock.

Where is the boutique?

Visit Shree Boutique at Rathtala Rd, Bardhaman, West Bengal 713102. Use the map link for directions, or message on WhatsApp before travelling.

Do you offer online ordering or delivery?

Please contact the boutique directly to ask what purchase and delivery options are currently available.

Are prices listed online?

Prices are not published on this site. Ask the boutique about any piece you are considering.

How can I follow new looks?

Follow the linked Shree Boutique Facebook page to see the latest public posts and videos.
